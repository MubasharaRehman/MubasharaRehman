## 👋 About Me

I am an **AI researcher working at the intersection of computer vision, deep learning, and medical image analysis**. I recently submitted my PhD thesis in *Artificial Intelligence and Data Analysis for Personalized Oncology* at the **University of Udine, Italy**.

My research focuses on developing deep learning and generative AI methods for **medical image reconstruction, enhancement, restoration, and cross-modality transformation** across CT and MRI.

My work has included:

* 🩻 **CT Metal Artifact Reduction** for radiotherapy applications
* 🔄 **Cross-Modality Image Translation** and CT domain transformation
* 🧠 **MRI Reconstruction and Enhancement**, including low-field to high-field MRI
* ✨ **Generative AI** for medical image restoration and reconstruction
* 🎯 **Image Enhancement and Restoration**
* 🔬 Deep learning methods including **Vision Transformers, Diffusion Models, and Flow Matching**

I have worked across academic, clinical, and industrial collaborations, including **IRCCS Centro di Riferimento Oncologico di Aviano (CRO)**, **Leiden University Medical Center (LUMC)**, and **Philips Research**.
